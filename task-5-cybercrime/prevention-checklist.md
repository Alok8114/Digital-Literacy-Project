# Stay Safe Online: A Cybersecurity Checklist for College Students

As digital natives, college students are frequent targets for cybercriminals. Follow these actionable steps to protect your personal data, reputation, and finances.

## General Online Safety & Privacy

1. **Lock Down Your Social Media:** Set your Instagram, Facebook, and other personal profiles to private. Only accept requests from people you know in real life to prevent identity theft and impersonation.
2. **Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA):** Turn on 2FA for all critical apps, especially WhatsApp, Gmail, and Instagram. This adds an essential layer of security even if your password is compromised.
3. **Beware of "Too Good to Be True" Offers:** Be highly skeptical of unsolicited WhatsApp messages or emails offering guaranteed internships, free laptops, or easy part-time jobs. Never pay a "registration fee" for a job interview.
4. **Avoid Public Wi-Fi for Sensitive Tasks:** Do not log into your bank accounts, enter passwords, or make payments while connected to free campus or café Wi-Fi. Use your mobile data instead.
5. **Beware of Video Call Scams:** Do not accept video calls from unknown numbers, especially late at night. This is a common tactic used in sextortion scams targeting young adults.
6. **Keep Your Devices Updated:** Regularly update your phone’s operating system and apps. These updates contain crucial security patches that protect against newly discovered vulnerabilities.

## Financial & UPI Safety

7. **Never Enter Your PIN to Receive Money:** Remember the golden rule of UPI: Your UPI PIN is **only** required when you are *sending* money or checking your balance. You never need to enter your PIN to *receive* a payment (e.g., when selling old books or a bicycle online).
8. **Verify the Receiver's Details:** Before hitting 'Send' or entering your PIN, always double-check the UPI ID and the registered name displayed on your screen. Scammers often use names that look similar to official businesses or friends.

---

## 🚨 Emergency Reporting Channels

If you or a peer fall victim to a cybercrime, act immediately:

* **National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal:** File a complaint online at [cybercrime.gov.in](https://cybercrime.gov.in)
* **National Cyber Crime Helpline:** Dial **1930** immediately. This 24/7 helpline is specifically designed to help freeze funds in cases of financial fraud before the scammers can withdraw them.
